# How to Cite

Citation formats for *Risk Management for Open-Weight Models in Enterprise*. Machine-readable
metadata is in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).

## Plain citation

> Microsoft and NVIDIA. *Risk Management for Open-Weight Models in Enterprise.*
> Edited by Andrew Berkley, April Chen, Michael Boone, Eileen Long, and Arun Rao.
> Version 1.0, October 2026.
> https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenEnterpriseRiskManagementGuidance

## Direct link to the paper

https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenEnterpriseRiskManagementGuidance/blob/main/Papers/Risk%20Management%20Guidance.pdf

To cite a specific revision rather than whatever is current on `main`, replace `main`
with the commit SHA from `Papers/CHANGELOG.md`. For version 1.0:

https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenEnterpriseRiskManagementGuidance/blob/cf06442df59758ab69d83d822ca1586d3e7dc2c4/Papers/Risk%20Management%20Guidance.pdf
